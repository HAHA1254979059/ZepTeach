Read `note-doctrine.md`.

`notes.py sections` lists the six required sections and what each absence
costs. `notes.py check` says whether a draft would be accepted before
writing it.

Both copies are written together: markdown for reading back, a document for
the learner.

Anything that records the conversation rather than the knowledge goes to the
journal with `notes.py journal`.
