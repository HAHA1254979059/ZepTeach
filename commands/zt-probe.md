---
description: Work out what this course can practise on, and what it cannot
---

Read `setup-stage2.md` and `practice-reach.md`.

`practice_reach.py check --course <slug>` reports what practice has to act on
and how close it can get. Report shortfalls in terms of the work, never in
terms of the missing thing.

Exit 3 means either a required target is out of reach, or the goal names
something no substitute covers. Offer exactly two ways forward: obtain it, or
change the goal. Do not offer a third.

Record what they have with `resources.py`, including how they use it.
Then `--write`.

When the check passes, start the first lesson. The learner asked to study
something; arriving at a green light and handing back a command to type is
where the momentum goes.
