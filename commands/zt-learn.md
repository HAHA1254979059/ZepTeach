description: Teach a lesson
argument-hint: "[--energy low|normal|high] [--minutes N]"
---

`session.py open` first, and read the brief before saying anything. It prints
how long it has been, what is overdue, which register applies, and how many
new concepts today allows.

Then `route.py for lesson --course <slug>` and read what it names.

Follow `session-protocol.md` in order. Do not skip the review step, and do
not teach when the brief says the backlog is severe.

Call `session.py turn` after every exchange.
